What is Analytical Exposition?

An analytical exposition is a text that presents the writer's opinion or argument about an issue and supports it with reasons.

🎯 Purpose

To persuade readers that an idea or opinion is important or reasonable.

💡 Topic

Usually discusses current or familiar issues.

🗣️ Main Idea

Opinion + reasons + reinforcement.

Language Features

Simple Present TenseOpinion verbsConnectivesCausal wordsPersuasive expressions

🧩 Text Structure

1️⃣ Thesis

Introduces the topic and states the writer's position.

2️⃣ Arguments

Presents reasons and explanations supporting the thesis.

3️⃣ Reiteration

Restates the writer's position and reinforces the argument.

Quick Check

Which part contains the reasons supporting the writer's opinion?

📖 Explore the Text

The Urgency of Digital Literacy for Modern Teenagers

THESIS

Digital literacy is essential for modern teenagers because they use digital technology for learning, communication, and entertainment every day.

ARGUMENT 1

First, digital literacy helps teenagers identify reliable information. The internet contains useful information, but not everything online is accurate.

ARGUMENT 2

Second, digital literacy encourages responsible online communication. Teenagers need to understand how their words and actions can affect others.

REITERATION

Therefore, digital literacy should become an important part of teenagers' education so they can use technology wisely and responsibly.

Connectives: because, First, but, Second, Therefore.
